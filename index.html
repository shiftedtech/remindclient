FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Is my data safe?
Yes. Every account's data is completely isolated — you can never see another person's clients, and they can never see yours. We use Google Cloud with server-side security. Your data never leaves Singapore's servers.
Do my clients need to install an app?
No. Reminders arrive via Telegram or WhatsApp — apps they already use. Calendar subscriptions work with iPhone or Android's built-in calendar. Zero downloads, zero setup for them.
Can I use this for something other than tutoring?
Absolutely. Anywhere you need to politely remind someone about a payment or an appointment — tenants, freelance clients, shared expenses, regular bookings — RemindClient handles it. The AI adapts to your context.
Can I write my own reminder messages?
Yes. The AI drafts a suggestion, but you can edit it, rewrite it, or type your own from scratch. You can also ask the AI to change the tone: "make it more formal", "translate to Malay", "add my PayNow QR link".
What happens after the free trial?
You pick a plan and pay via PayNow (bank transfer coming soon). If you don't pick a plan, your account simply stops sending reminders — your data stays safe and accessible for 30 days.
I run an organisation, not a solo practice. Does this work for us?
Yes — the Centre plan is built for exactly this. Each staff member gets their own login and sees only their own clients. The admin sees everyone: all staff, all clients, all payments, one dashboard.
What about WhatsApp auto-send?
We currently support Telegram for automatic sending and WhatsApp for one-tap manual send. Full WhatsApp Business API auto-send is coming soon and will be included in the Centre plan at no extra cost.
